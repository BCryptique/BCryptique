## What I do

- **Find the process problem first.** I map the steps, count the errors and the hours, and decide whether a tool will help. Sometimes a checklist is the answer.
- **Build small, tested tools.** Scripts, calculators, schedulers, and data checks that a non-programmer can run.
- **Keep data private.** I prefer tools that run on your own machine and send nothing out.
- **Prove it.** I write down what I checked and what I could not check.

## How I use AI

- I use AI to draft, search, and review. I do not publish an AI answer I have not checked.
- Facts get a source. If I cannot verify a fact, I label it "not verified" instead of guessing.
- I use cheaper models for bulk reading and a stronger model to check the result.
- I keep private data on my own machine when I can.
