# AI process improvement for small teams

I help small businesses and offices find paperwork steps that waste time and fix them with simple, tested tools. I start small and I show my work.

## Who this is for

- Offices with manual intake, scheduling, or records steps
- Teams that keep data in spreadsheets and paper
- Organizations that want AI help but do not want to send private data to a cloud service

## What you get

| Step | What I do | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Process review (1 to 2 hours) | I map one process, step by step. I count errors, hours, and handoffs. | A one-page map and a list of problems ranked by cost. |
| 2. Recommendation | I say what to fix, what to leave alone, and what tool, if any, fits. | A short written plan with a time and cost estimate. |
| 3. Build | I make a small tool or template, such as a script, a checklist, a form, or a data check. | A working tool with instructions and tests. |
| 4. Check | I test it on your real examples and record what I could not check. | A test log. |
| 5. Handoff | I train your staff and write a short procedure. | A procedure guide, in plain language. |

## Examples of work I can do

- Reconcile inventory or billing records and flag mismatches (I recovered $168,573 in a 340-work-order audit).
- Replace a manual intake step with a database (I raised a law firm's intake to 500+ per month).
- Write the procedure manual new staff use on day one (a 16-page guide was adopted across stores).
- Automate a daily or nightly job on Windows: reports, backups, calendar sync.
- Draft and check documents with AI, with every fact tied to a source.

## How I handle your data

- I work on your machine or a local one when I can.
- I keep passwords and private links out of code.
- I use AI tools only on data you approve in writing.
- I tell you when I cannot verify a result.
